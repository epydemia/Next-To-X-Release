EV charging on the motorway in Switzerland

Next-To-X knows 217 fast-charging locations along the motorways in Switzerland: 31 at service areas and 186 just off an exit.

Where to charge

In Switzerland you charge at motorway service areas and at many filling stations just off the exit.

Operators with the most locations

GOFAST (62), Tesla (33), evpass (15), IONITY (14), MOVE (13), M-Charge (10), Migrol (10), EVite (9), Shell Recharge (4), Swisscharge (4)

Tolls and vignettes

You need the annual motorway vignette, which you can also buy online as an e-vignette. There are no other tolls, except for a few tunnels such as the Great St Bernard.

Paying for charging

Many new fast chargers take contactless credit and debit cards directly. Otherwise you pay with the operator's app or with a roaming charge card: before you leave, check that your app or card also works in the country you are going to.

Tips for the trip

  • Always keep some range in reserve: a charger can be broken, busy or switched off.
  • On summer weekends and at the start of the holidays there can be queues at chargers on the main routes.
  • Before you stop, check in the operator's app that the charger is free and working.
  • Chargers off an exit mean leaving the motorway: allow a little extra time and, where there is one, the toll.

How Next-To-X helps

Next-To-X is a free web app: it follows your position and direction of travel and shows the next fast charger on your side of the motorway, with the distance, without searching. Nothing to install, no account.
