Along the A13 Next-To-X knows 5 fast-charging locations: 3 at service areas and 2 just off an exit.

How Next-To-X helps

Next-To-X is a free web app: it follows your position and direction of travel and shows the next fast charger on your side of the motorway, with the distance, without searching. Nothing to install, no account.
