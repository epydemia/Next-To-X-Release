EV charging on the motorway in Italy

Next-To-X knows 315 fast-charging locations along the motorways in Italy: 160 at service areas and 155 just off an exit.

Where to charge

Fast chargers are found at many service areas (area di servizio): on the Autostrade per l'Italia network mostly Free To X, and on the A22 Brenner motorway also the motorway company's own. Many more are just off an exit, for example at hotels and shopping centres: to use them you leave the motorway.

Operators with the most locations

Free To X (105), Tesla (72), Enel X Way (30), IONITY (27), Autostrada del Brennero (20), Alperia (6), Atlante (6), Electra (6), Plenitude (6), Be Charge (5)

Tolls and vignettes

In Italy the toll depends on the distance driven: you usually take a ticket when you join the motorway and pay when you leave, in cash, by card or with an electronic toll tag (Telepass and similar). If you leave to charge, you pay up to that exit and take a new ticket when you rejoin.

Paying for charging

Many new fast chargers take contactless credit and debit cards directly. Otherwise you pay with the operator's app or with a roaming charge card: before you leave, check that your app or card also works in the country you are going to.

Tips for the trip

  • Always keep some range in reserve: a charger can be broken, busy or switched off.
  • On summer weekends and at the start of the holidays there can be queues at chargers on the main routes.
  • Before you stop, check in the operator's app that the charger is free and working.
  • Chargers off an exit mean leaving the motorway: allow a little extra time and, where there is one, the toll.

How Next-To-X helps

Next-To-X is a free web app: it follows your position and direction of travel and shows the next fast charger on your side of the motorway, with the distance, without searching. Nothing to install, no account.
