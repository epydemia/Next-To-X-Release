Before you set off

  • 📵 Don't use your phone while driving: hand it to a passenger or put it in a holder.
  • ⚡ The data is indicative: a charger may be missing, out of order or busy. Check the operator's app and keep some range in reserve.
  • 🔒 Your location is never stored. It is used on your phone; off the motorway the coordinates are sent to OpenStreetMap only to get the road name.
  • 📍 Next, your browser will ask for your location: tap Allow, it's how the app finds the chargers ahead of you.
  • ℹ️ Independent app, not affiliated with Free To X, Autostrade per l'Italia, Autostrada del Brennero or any operator mentioned.
About and privacy

📍 Your location is needed

Next-To-X follows your location to work out which motorway you are on and which way you are going, and to show you the next charger on your side of the road.

Your location is only used on your phone: we never receive or store it.

When you tap, your browser will ask for permission: choose Allow.
